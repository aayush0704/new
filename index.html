I'm So Sorry
I should have been there. I wasn't. I'm sorry.
My love,

I owe you more than an apology — I owe you an honest confession. I pulled away. I went quiet. I let the distance grow between us when you deserved the exact opposite — someone fully present, fully there for you.

I know how that must have felt. To reach out and feel like no one was really reaching back. To wonder if I still cared, when the truth is — I care more than I know how to say.
"You were not asking for too much. I was giving too little. That was my failure, not yours."
You have never once made me feel unseen. You show up — always — with your whole heart. You deserved that same energy back, every single day. I let you down, and I am deeply sorry for that.

From this moment on, I choose to show up. To listen. To hold your hand when you need it. To be the person you see in me, even when I forget to see it in myself. 💛
